## Brand & Style

This design system channels the quiet, vast stillness of deep cosmic space intersected with advanced astronomical instrumentation. It serves elite engineers, creative technologists, and digital artisans presenting high-complexity work where precision and quiet confidence are paramount.

The emotional signature is an expansive, hypnotic calm: low-friction visual environments, luminous focal points that pull the eye inward, and high-spec technical elegance.

The aesthetic fuses **Dark Space Minimalism** with **Refined Technical Glassmorphism**:
- Obsidian, pitch-black canvases layered over subtle interstellar gradations.
- Prismatic nebular accents (cyan, celestial purple, and ultraviolet) reserved exclusively for high-intent actions, active states, and focal glows.
- Micro-fine hairline borders evoking precision telescope reticles and technical optical viewfinders.
- Controlled atmospheric blur that simulates starlight refracting through frosted silica glass.

## Colors

The color palette simulates the depth of deep space: infinite baseline blacks overlaid with celestial illumination.

- **Background Canvas (`#07080d`):** The absolute void. Used for underlying viewports, document bodies, and deep negative space.
- **Surface Elevation (`#0b0d13` to `#111420`):** Stratified layers representing structural spatial volume.
- **Primary / Nebula Cyan (`#38bdf8`):** Cold starlight accent. Dictates primary visual anchors, cursor interactions, progress status, and dominant CTAs.
- **Secondary / Deep Violet (`#a855f7`):** Gravitational energy. Used for secondary highlights, ambient gradient meshes, and selected states.
- **Tertiary / Celestial Indigo (`#6366f1`):** Bridging tone between cyan and purple; ideal for interactive focus rings, tag fills, and technical data visualization.
- **Star-Dust Accents (`#e2e8f0` to `#ffffff` at varying opacities):** Pristine optical white used for crisp vector typography, active hairline dividers, and pin-point reticle markers.
- **Border / Horizon Rims (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`):** Ultra-fine architectural outlines evoking laser etching and optical instrument glass.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances futuristic aerospace telemetry with high-readability editorial presentation.

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Chosen for its engineered geometric bones, technical cuts, and assertive modernism. Applied to titles, showcase case studies, and quantitative metrics.
- **Body & Prose (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Offers a crisp, human, balanced geometric profile that maintains optical purity and extreme legibility against pitch-black luminous backgrounds.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Metadata (`Space Mono`):** Delivers precise instrumentation cues. Used for dates, project tags, coordinates, indices, and architectural notations. Always set with expanded tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an expansive **12-column fluid grid** flanked by structural margins that simulate panoramic wide-angle astronomical observation decks.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `3rem` boundary margins. Content max-width is constrained to `1440px` to maintain strict optical focal paths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer padding. Side-by-side technical panels collapse into dual split columns.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` canvas margins. Asymmetrical and modular showcases stack vertically into telemetry cards.

Component spacing obeys an 8-point rhythmic pulse (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`), ensuring intentional empty vacuum between disparate modular components to evoke deep atmospheric distance.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth does not rely on traditional dropped shadows. Instead, visual hierarchy is achieved through **optical luminescent stratification**:

1. **Layer 0 (Canvas Void):** `#07080d` deep static background, punctuated only by ambient radial nebula gradients (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08), transparent 70%)`).
2. **Layer 1 (Subsurface Chassis):** `#0b0d13` with a flat 1px borderline of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Glassmorphic Stardust Panels):** Background rendered as `rgba(17, 20, 32, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`. Border is a compound hairline: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
4. **Layer 3 (Active Hover / Luminescent Float):** Backdrop opacity increases to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` top specular highlight. Borders gain a subtle starlight edge: `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`.
5. **Cosmic Halo Emissives:** Active interactive components emit non-directional nebular blooms (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25), 0 0 48px -12px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (`1`)** shape archetype. Precision instrumentation demands crispness; rounded geometries are tightly controlled to mirror calibrated scientific hardware, optical prisms, and high-performance screens rather than organic consumer curves.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem`):** Applied to badges, chips, code spans, micro buttons, and form check elements.
- **Large Radius (`0.5rem`):** Applied to interactive buttons, text inputs, modal alerts, and modular showcase cards.
- **Extra Large Radius (`0.75rem`):** Reserved strictly for master viewport panels, hero presentation displays, and docked glass floating navigation bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Gradient or solid cyan backdrop (`#38bdf8`) with jet-black typography (`#07080d`, `Space Grotesk` weight 600). Hover triggers a localized diffuse cyan starlight glow (`0 0 20px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Telemetry:** Translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), 1px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text in `#e2e8f0`. On hover, the border shifts to `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.5)` with an interior purple nebula tint.
- **Icon Buttons:** Fixed square aspect ratios matching the standard spacing scale (`space-xl` or `2.5rem`), centered vector icon, hairline glass treatment.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Compact capsules utilizing `Space Mono` at `label-sm` or `label-md`.
- Background: `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.24)`.
- Left-aligned micro status indicator: 4px circular LED dot glowing in static `#38bdf8` or pulsing `#a855f7`.

### Cards & Project Showcase Nodes
- Structured with layered glassmorphism: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, background `rgba(17, 20, 32, 0.6)`.
- Star-dust hairline perimeter: Top edge highlighted with a subtle gradient (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255,255,255,0.15), transparent)`), remaining edges `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`.
- Hover triggers smooth cursor-following radial illumination and an elevation transition to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` background brightness.

### Input Fields
- Inset cockpit aesthetic: Background `rgba(7, 8, 13, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Text set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`#f8fafc`), with placeholder labels in muted space slate (`#64748b`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#38bdf8` accompanied by an ambient internal field glow (`0 0 12px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp technical profile (`roundedness: 1`).
- Inactive: Inset dark field (`#0b0d13`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Active: Background `#38bdf8`, with black check vector or centered glowing ultraviolet core for radio indicators.

### Terminal & Metric Panels
- Distinctive portfolio addition: Specialized monospaced technical metric strips displaying live availability, stack capabilities, or GitHub commits.
- Uses `Space Mono` typography, strict hairline tabular dividers, and faint cyan scanline overlays across card surfaces.
